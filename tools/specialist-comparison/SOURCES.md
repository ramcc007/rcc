## Why the numbers differ from the original slide 9 screenshot
That screenshot captured the 15 July snapshot, not August. Refreshing to 15
August puts this slide on the same date basis as the vendor comparison. Notable
movements between the two dates:

| Domain | Keywords Jul to Aug | AI answers Jul to Aug |
|---|---|---|
| komprise.com | 6,383 to 6,139 | 5,532 to 5,464 |
| unstructured.io | 1,960 to 2,221 | 1,346 to 1,789 |
| hammerspace.com | 819 to 853 | 380 to 528 |
| atempo.com | 322 to 219 | 232 to 167 |
| datadobi.com | 86 to 115 | 64 to 95 |
| diskoverdata.com | 82 to 82 | 54 to 49 |

Hammerspace grew AI answer presence by 39 per cent and Unstructured.io by 33 per
cent over those four weeks, while Komprise edged down by about one per cent.
That is the basis for the second read-out panel.

## Traffic column

September 2026, marked with an asterisk. The other six stay on Semrush
estimates, because Search Console data exists only for owned properties. The
same treatment and footnote are used on the vendor comparison slide. On a
like-for-like Semrush basis Komprise would read 25,126.
